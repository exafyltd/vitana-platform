# Requirements

1. Mariia, Dragan and Jovana can maintain a private shared calendar for Let’s Dance, awards, performances and appearances.
2. The branded `/maxina/events` route and the Commerce link let invited partners find the same schedule.
3. Partners can filter events, switch between chronological list and month views, and browse only events cleared for this audience.
4. A partner can submit a product or service contribution with quantity or capacity and delivery timing. The team can review the proposal and update its status.
5. The page works on desktop and mobile, supports keyboard navigation and uses the official MAXINA brand asset. Treat missing dates and photos explicitly.
