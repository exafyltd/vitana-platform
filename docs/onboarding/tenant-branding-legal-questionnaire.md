# Welcome to Vitanaland: your community onboarding questionnaire

Thank you for joining Vitanaland. To set up your own community space (a tailored
clone of the Maxina app) we need a few details about **how your community looks**
(branding) and **who runs it legally** (legal). Please fill in the answers below
and send this document back to the Exafy team, along with your image files.

You do not need any technical knowledge. Every question has a plain-language
prompt, the format we need, and a real example from an existing community
(Maxina or Alkalma) so you can see what a good answer looks like. If a question
does not apply to you, just write "n/a" and we will help.

The Exafy team then transcribes your answers into the provisioning pack and runs
the manual setup. You do not send this anywhere else, and you do not set anything
up yourself: we do that for you.

---

## How to fill this in

- Replace each `→ Your answer:` line with your answer.

## Part A: Branding

This is everything that makes the community look and sound like yours: the name,
colours, logo, the address members write to for help, and the facts your
assistant should know about you.

### A1. Community short name (slug)

The short, lowercase handle for your community. It becomes part of your web
address and cannot be changed later, so choose carefully.

- **Format:** 3 to 32 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens
  only (for example `green-circle`, not `Green Circle` or `green--circle`). It
  must not be a reserved word; the Exafy team will confirm your choice is free.
- **Example:** `maxina` · `alkalma`

The name shown to members in the app. This one can use capital letters and
spaces exactly as you want it to appear.

- **Format:** short text, up to 80 characters.

## What happens next

Once you send this back:

1. The Exafy team reviews your answers (including a quick check that the persona
   facts are facts, not spoken lines).
2. We transcribe your answers into the provisioning pack and run the manual setup
   described in our internal runbook
   (`docs/validation/VTID-05150/RUNBOOK-provision.md`).
3. We set up your branding, your assistant and your community space, then confirm
   with you when it is ready.

A few fields are **set by the Exafy team, not by you**: things like the admin
email, AI budget, enabled languages, timezone and currency. We will confirm those
with you directly during setup, so you do not need to provide them here.

Thank you. We are looking forward to welcoming your community to Vitanaland.
