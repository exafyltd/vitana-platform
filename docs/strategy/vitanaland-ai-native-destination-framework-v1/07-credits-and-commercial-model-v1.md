# Vitana Credits and Commercial Model V1

**Status:** Commercial/technical design baseline; pricing not approved. **Version:** 0.1.

## 1. Purpose
Provide a transparent way to pay for AI-assisted creation and operation of destinations. Participants see estimates, approve material work, monitor progress and reconcile actual usage.

## 2. Three distinct cost categories
**Vitana AI Credits:** prepaid service credits for reasoning, planning, content generation, knowledge processing, coding/configuration, integration setup, testing and AI-assisted maintenance.

**Business subscription:** recurring access to destination/business capabilities and shared platform services, with separately defined entitlements.

**External provider costs:** hosting, domains, payment processing, booking, email, messaging, storage and other third-party services. Identify who bills and whether Vitanaland resells or passes through.

Never combine these into one opaque balance.

## 3. Credits are not VTNA
Vitana Credits are prepaid service credits. VTNA is a separate ecosystem token with independent tokenomics, legal and regulatory treatment. No conversion, redemption or utility relationship is assumed. Any future linkage requires separate approval.

## 4. Ledger model
Conceptual records: account owner, available/reserved balance, posted consumption, adjustments/refunds, immutable transaction ledger, validity terms, limits/alerts and entitlement references. Balance must reconcile to auditable entries; do not rely on an unexplained mutable number.

## 5. Estimate-to-settlement workflow
Intent → plan and estimate → disclose assumptions/range/external costs → check budget → reserve if needed → obtain approval → execute within scope → report actual consumption → release unused reservation/reconcile variance → store evidence and attribution. Approval binds to plan/estimate revision; material scope or cost change requires renewed approval.

## 6. Budget controls
Support per-task, per-destination, periodic and organization limits; alerts; approval thresholds; pause at limit; and explicit continuation policy. State clearly whether a limit covers AI credits only or also third-party spending.

## 7. Metering
Attribute tenant, organization, destination, actor/agent, plan/task/run, model/provider, usage units, tool operations, worker usage if billable, pricing version, estimate/reservation/actual and external charges separately. Pricing changes are versioned.

## 8. Long-running work
Support pause/resume/cancel/status where safe. Cancellation settles completed work under published terms and stops remaining work. It does not imply reversal of already-completed external actions.

## 9. Subscription entitlements
Potential entitlements: number of destinations, platform capabilities, storage/traffic, seats, integration access, analytics, support and service levels. Tier names and prices are open; architecture must not depend on a fixed tier catalogue.

## 10. External cost disclosure
Before enabling paid providers, identify provider/service, direct-vs-pass-through billing, recurring/usage-based costs, currency/tax/interval where known, cancellation/dependency implications and required consent. Never silently activate unexpected paid services.

## 11. Refunds and disputes
Define treatment for failed execution, duplicate metering, partial completion, cancellation, provider disputes, purchase reversal and service outage. Adjust the ledger with reason/evidence; do not erase history. Do not promise refund terms before approval.

## 12. Managed, BYOI and Hybrid
- **Managed:** Vitanaland selects/manages infrastructure; recommended for simplicity.
- **BYOI:** participant owns selected provider accounts/infrastructure.
- **Hybrid:** shared responsibility.

Specify account ownership, billing, backups, support, updates, security, export and termination. This is a responsibility model, not just a UI toggle.

## 13. Acceptance criteria
Credits/subscription/provider charges separate; estimates and actuals attributable; limits enforceable; material changes approved; long jobs controllable; ledger auditable; provider billing clear; VTNA separate; infrastructure responsibilities explicit.

## 14. Open decisions
Credit denomination/rates; margin and model cost allocation; reserve/settlement; expiry/refunds; tiers/entitlements; budgets; tax/invoicing/currency/jurisdictions; provider contracts; BYOI support; legal classification.

**Guardrail:** no prices, financial products, token linkage or legal terms are approved by this document. Obtain commercial and legal review before launch.
