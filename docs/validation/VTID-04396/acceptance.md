## How the screenshots were made

`outputs/harness-server.js` serves the working-tree statics and answers the seven orchestrator endpoints from fixtures:
- **Live rows:** VTID-04354's live-rows fixture.
- **Budgets, shadow and delegations:** `orchestrator-panels-fixture.json`, generated by running the real `budgets.ts` / `policy.ts` / `tool-catalog.ts` / `dispatcher.ts` modules locally.

Nothing live was called and nothing was written. `harness-shoot.js` shoots 1400×900 and 390×844 and records its findings in `shoot-result.json`.

One fixture artefact: the budgets fixture attaches each agent's whole daily spend to a single VTID, so 3 lines read "over". That is a property of the fixture, not of live spend.
