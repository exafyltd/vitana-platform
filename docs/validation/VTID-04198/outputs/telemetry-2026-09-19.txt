=== The client-side fault is invisible here, by construction ===
_announceAudioBlocked() sets the orb to 'paused', shows a tap-to-hear caption,
and sends NOTHING to the gateway. That is why every server-side diagnostic for
58373903 reports a healthy session while the user heard silence — and it is
what VTID-04198 changes.
